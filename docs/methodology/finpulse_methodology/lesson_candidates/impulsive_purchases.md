# Lesson Candidate: Impulsive Purchases

Source references: `../00_original_content.md` lines 74-129, 131-230, 821-843.

This is a focused methodology slice for the source lesson
"Импульсивные покупки". It preserves the lesson intent and card sequence as
editorial source, not runtime JSON.

## Learning Goal

The user understands that an impulsive purchase is not just weak willpower. It is
a combination of trigger, emotion, context, and absence of a rule.

## Lesson Shape

Use the short lesson formula:

1. Hook: a life situation with a tempting purchase.
2. Mini-theory: one idea about emotions, advertising, pressure, or context.
3. Understanding practice: distinguish optional from required spending.
4. Real-world check: inspect final price, delivery, return terms.
5. Personal reflection: identify a trigger behind a recent impulse.
6. Micro-rule: choose a pause rule for the next week.

## Source Card Sequence

### Card 1. Memory

Question: which expense is optional?

Options:

- rent;
- prescribed medicine;
- a new phone case while the old one is intact;
- utility payments.

Best answer: the new phone case.

Develops: hard skills.

### Card 2. Understanding

Situation: the user has a goal to save 30,000 rubles for vacation. A store runs a
"today only" promotion, and the purchase costs 4,500 rubles.

Best answer: compare the price in two places and return to the decision tomorrow.

Feedback idea: a discount is not automatically a benefit. It helps only if the
thing is truly needed and does not damage the financial goal.

Develops: understanding + psychology.

### Card 3. Real World

Task: open any marketplace or online store without buying anything and find:

- final price;
- delivery cost;
- return terms.

Follow-up question: did the product-card price match the final cost?

Develops: checking information, attention, and working with conditions.

### Card 4. Personal World

Task: remember the last impulsive purchase. Exact amount is not required; use a
range:

- up to 500 rubles;
- 500-2,000 rubles;
- 2,000-10,000 rubles;
- more than 10,000 rubles.

Prompt: what influenced the purchase most?

Source options:

- discount;
- stress;
- boredom;
- desire to reward oneself;
- social pressure;
- fear of missing out;
- other.

Develops: psychology.

### Card 5. Habit

Choose a rule for the week:

```text
Покупки дороже ___ ₽ я отправляю в список желаний и возвращаюсь к ним через 24 часа.
```

Alternative:

```text
Когда я хочу купить что-то незапланированное, я сначала спрашиваю: какую цель это отодвинет?
```

Develops: habits.

## Design Notes

- Avoid shame. The source repeatedly frames mistakes as training, not failure.
- Use amount ranges instead of requiring exact sensitive sums.
- Keep the final behavior rule small and repeatable.
- If adapted to runtime, preserve the source distinction between optional expense,
  discount pressure, final-cost checking, personal trigger, and pause rule.
